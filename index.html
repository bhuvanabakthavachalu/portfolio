Case Study · Healthcare & Dental Marketing · Self-Directed Strategic Rebuild

Rebuilding Apollo Dental's acquisition strategy as an always-on, multi-city campaign

An independent rebuild of my dental-industry ad plan — evergreen rather than seasonal, phased across 5 real Apollo Dental cities, with a real technical SEO audit and compliance considerations most fresher plans skip.

Role Campaign Strategist
Sector Healthcare · Dental
Structure Always-on, multi-city
Platforms Google Ads · Meta Ads
Geography 5 Indian metros
Year 2026
Note: Apollo Dental (apollodental.in) is a real, operating dental chain — part of Apollo Hospitals Group, with 220+ locations across 45 Indian cities. This is a self-directed educational project built around their real, public-facing business and website. It is not an actual client engagement or an Apollo Dental–commissioned campaign.
01 — The Brief & Objective

Building the best ad plan for a healthcare business

This started as a course exercise: prepare the best ad plan for a healthcare business, with a suggested budget, promoted across India, including Avg. CPC, Cost/Conversion, and expected Leads. I rebuilt it from scratch as I'd approach it professionally — an always-on plan rather than a seasonal push, phased geographically instead of spread thin nationwide on day one.

Objective: generate qualified appointment leads for Apollo Dental across 5 real clinic cities, at a sustainable and improving cost-per-lead. Primary KPI: Cost per Lead (CPL), tracked weekly by city and channel. Secondary KPI: lead-to-booked-appointment rate.

02 — SWOT & Competitive Landscape

Where Apollo Dental stands

Strengths

Trusted brand with pan-India presence, advanced technology and skilled professionals, strong testimonials and word-of-mouth.

Weaknesses

Perceived as costlier than local clinics, limited focus on younger/aesthetic-seeking demographics, dependence on promotions.

Opportunities

Rising oral health awareness in India, growing demand for aesthetic dentistry, potential wellness/influencer partnerships.

Threats

Local and international chains expanding into India, volatile digital ad costs, unpredictable healthcare spending.

03 — Audience & Targeting

Who we're targeting

Core audience

Adults 25–55, urban India, moderate-to-high disposable income. Targeted on demographics and general wellness/lifestyle interests only — not gender-specific.

Secondary audience

Parents/household decision-makers searching for family dental care — often the highest-value segment.

Compliance note: Meta's advertising policy prohibits targeting or messaging that asserts or implies a health condition or need — including inferred ones. Targeting here is demographic and interest-based only, never on inferred health status.
04 — Geographic Rollout Strategy

"Promote across India" doesn't mean spend evenly, day one

A brief that says "promote across India" doesn't mean spend evenly nationwide from launch — that wastes budget generating leads in cities with no clinic to convert them.

Phase 1 · Months 1–3

Concentrate in real clinic cities

100% of budget across Apollo Dental's 5 established metros — Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad. Establish real CPL and show-up-rate benchmarks before spending anywhere else.

Phase 2 · Month 4+

Expand using real data

Reallocate budget toward whichever cities beat their estimated CPL before adding new geographies — evidence-led expansion, not a guess.

The creative hook

Effective healthcare advertising avoids clinical coldness.

11 — Measurement & Key Learnings

What I'd track, and what I'd do differently

Weekly: CPL by channel/city, lead-to-booked-appointment rate, Search Impression Share. Call tracking: both the Meta retarget path and Search's call extension drive phone calls — a unique tracking number per channel/city is needed before launch.

1

Splitting spend across platforms diversifies risk. No single platform's cost swings sink the whole campaign.

2

Campaign objective and creative CTA need to match. A "Leads" objective paired with a "Call now" CTA is a real mismatch I found reviewing my own earlier work — fixed here with a proper native Lead Form.

3

"Promote across India" isn't the same as spending evenly across India. Phasing spend into real clinic cities first satisfies the brief without wasting budget on markets with no clinic to convert the lead.

12 — Compliance Flags Raised Before Launch

What I'd route to legal before going live

A marketer's job is to flag these risks and route them to legal/compliance, not personally resolve them.

Dental Council of India Code of Ethics

DCI regulations restrict advertising for the purpose of soliciting patients; State Dental Councils have issued notices against clinics for this, including social media advertising. Sign-off needed on ad copy — brand-level messaging, no superlative claims.

DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

Healthcare entities are data fiduciaries requiring explicit, informed consent to collect personal data. Lead forms need a consent checkbox and privacy notice link before going live.

Case Study · Fitness & Wellness · Local Brand · Chennai

Building a loyal yoga community for Team Fitness through hyperlocal digital marketing

A full-funnel digital campaign designed to grow trial class bookings, build brand awareness, and create a returning member community for Team Fitness with Yoga — a boutique yoga studio in Porur, Chennai.

RoleCampaign Strategist
BrandTeam Fitness with Yoga
LocationPorur, Chennai
SectorFitness · Wellness
PlatformsMeta · Google · WhatsApp
Year2025
Note: This is a self-initiated strategic case study built as part of my freelance outreach to a real local business — not a paid client engagement. Business details (reviews, Instagram content, service specialization) are drawn from the studio's actual public presence; campaign figures below are projections built from public benchmarks, not delivered results.
01 · The Brief

The challenge

Team Fitness with Yoga is a boutique, women's-health-focused yoga studio in Porur, Chennai — specializing in weight loss, hormone balancing, postpartum fitness, PCOS/PCOD, and fertility support. It's a real business with 124 Google reviews and a genuinely active Instagram, running recurring 30/60/100-day weight-loss challenges with strong organic testimonials and a WhatsApp-first coaching model.

That's exactly what makes this an interesting strategic problem: the studio has real organic traction but no systematized paid acquisition layer. The brief was to build a structured digital funnel around what's already working — not start from zero, but professionalize and scale an already-successful organic playbook.

Challenge 01

Strong organics, no paid funnel

124 Google reviews and an active Instagram full of transformation testimonials — but growth depends entirely on organic reach, with no paid acquisition layer to scale it deliberately.

Challenge 02

Hyperlocal reach needed

Budget was limited — every rupee had to reach people within 5 km who could realistically attend. No wasted national impressions.

Challenge 03

High churn in fitness

The fitness industry sees 50%+ dropout in the first 3 months. The campaign needed a retention layer, not just acquisition.

02 · The Strategy

4-stage hyperlocal funnel

Unlike a national brand campaign, this required a hyperlocal strategy — combining paid ads, organic content, Google My Business, and WhatsApp community building to win within a 5 km radius of Porur.

Discover

Systematize what's already working

Google Business Profile is live with 124 reviews but underused for weekly posts/photos. Meta ads with 5 km radius targeting in Porur, Ramapuram, and Valasaravakkam — layered on top of, not replacing, the organic reach already driving bookings. Targeting: women 24–45 interested in weight loss, hormone health, and postpartum fitness.

Engage

Build trust through organic content

Instagram Reels showing real class sessions, instructor introductions, and student transformation stories. Content pillars: yoga tips, behind the scenes, member spotlights, Chennai-specific content.

Convert

Drive trial bookings with a low-risk offer

Meta Lead Ads offering a free first class — removing the financial risk of trying a new studio. WhatsApp Click-to-Chat ads for instant enquiry resolution, matching the studio's real, already-working booking channel. Retargeting anyone who engaged with Reels but didn't book.

Retain

Build a community that keeps members coming back

WhatsApp broadcast group for members with weekly schedules and motivational content. Monthly "Member of the Month" feature on Instagram. Referral programme: bring a friend, get one class free.

Why hyperlocal changes everything: a national brand targets millions with a ₹10L budget. A local studio targets 50,000 people within 5 km with ₹15,000/month — and wins by being more relevant and more present in the neighbourhood than any national chain can be.

03 · Audience

Three personas, one real niche

The studio's real specialization — weight loss, hormone balancing, postpartum fitness, PCOS/PCOD, and fertility support — points at a sharper, more defensible audience than generic "yoga for fitness."

The Weight-Loss Committer

  • Age 28–45, drawn to the real 30/60/100-day challenge format
  • Motivated by visible, tracked progress
  • Best platform: Instagram Reels + WhatsApp
  • Hook: "Real results, real Porur women"

The Postpartum/Hormone-Health Seeker

  • Age 25–40, dealing with PCOS, thyroid, or postpartum recovery
  • Seeks a specialist, not a generic gym
  • Best platform: Facebook feed + Google Search
  • Hook: "Yoga built for your hormones"

The Fitness Explorer

  • Age 20–28, tried gym, wants something different
  • Price-sensitive, social media native
  • Best platform: Instagram Stories + Reels
  • Hook: "Not your grandmother's yoga"
04 · Content Strategy

Systematizing what's already working

The studio's real Instagram already runs on testimonials and challenge campaigns — the content plan here is about giving that a paid-media multiplier, not replacing it.

Pillar 01 · Amplify

Boost real testimonials

Existing before/after posts (e.g. "Lost 8.7kgs in 60 days") already convert organically — paid boosting extends their reach past existing followers.

Pillar 02 · Educate

Women's-health content

Content addressing PCOS, hormone balance, and postpartum recovery — the studio's real specialization, currently under-leveraged in messaging.

Pillar 03 · Challenge-driven

Support the 30/60/100-day challenges

The studio's real recurring challenge format is already a proven conversion mechanism — Meta ads timed to challenge launch dates capture new-cohort demand.

Pillar 04 · Local

Chennai-specific content

"Yoga before Porur traffic," "Beat the Chennai heat with evening yin yoga."

05 · Creative Direction

The creative hooks

Three different hooks for three different personas — same brand, different emotional entry points.

"Find Your Balance — Right Here in Porur"

The local name "Porur" in the headline signals extreme relevance to the target audience, and tells the Meta algorithm exactly who to show it to.

Secondary hooks: "Real results, real Porur women" · "Yoga built for your hormones" · "Not your grandmother's yoga"

1

Hyperlocal copy outperforms generic fitness ads. Mentioning "Porur" in the ad headline increases relevance score — the audience knows this is for them, not a national chain.

2

WhatsApp is India's most underused retention tool. A WhatsApp broadcast group costs ₹0 and retains members better than paid retargeting, because it feels personal, not promotional.
