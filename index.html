Case Study · Healthcare & Dental Marketing

Strategic patient acquisition for Apollo Dental through multi-stage funnel optimization

A comprehensive performance marketing strategy designed to increase qualified appointment bookings through targeted positioning across Meta and Google platforms, leveraging demographic and psychographic segmentation of female healthcare consumers in urban and semi-urban India.

Role Campaign Strategist
Sector Healthcare · Dental
Platforms Meta Ads · Google Ads
Geography Tier 1 & Tier 2 India
Year 2025
01 — The Brief

The challenge

Apollo Dental Clinic needed to increase appointment bookings among women aged 25–45 in urban and semi-urban India. The dental care category suffers from low awareness and high purchase hesitation — most people only visit a dentist when in pain, not for preventive care.

The campaign goal was to shift perception: from reactive ("I go when it hurts") to proactive ("My smile is worth investing in") — and drive measurable action through paid digital channels.

02 — The Strategy

Full-funnel campaign architecture

Rather than running a single promotional ad, I designed a 3-stage funnel to guide potential patients from awareness to booking — matching message intensity to audience readiness.

Awareness

Build emotional connection at the top

Meta Reels and Google Display ads using aspirational visuals and the core hook "Let Your Smile Do the Talking." Goal: reach cold audiences and create positive brand association with Apollo Dental.

Consider

Educate and build trust in the middle

Retargeted carousel ads featuring before/after results, doctor credentials, and patient testimonials. Google Search ads targeting high-intent keywords like "best dentist near me" and "teeth whitening Chennai."

Convert

Drive appointments at the bottom

Conversion-optimised Meta Lead Ads with a pre-filled appointment booking form. Urgency messaging ("Book your free consultation this week") and a strong CTA to reduce friction at the final step.

Tools used
Meta Ads Manager Google Ads Google Keyword Planner Canva Adobe Premiere Pro Google Analytics 4 Meta Audience Insights
03 — Audience

Who we targeted

Audience segmentation was built around two distinct personas with different pain points and messaging needs.

Persona A — The Urban Professional

  • ✓ Women, 28–38, metro cities
  • ✓ High disposable income
  • ✓ Motivated by appearance & confidence
  • ✓ Responsive to aspirational messaging
  • ✓ Platform: Meta (Instagram feed & Reels)

Persona B — The Health-Conscious Mother

  • ✓ Women, 32–45, Tier 2 cities
  • ✓ Family health decision-maker
  • ✓ Motivated by trust & safety
  • ✓ Responsive to doctor credibility
  • ✓ Platform: Google Search + Meta Feed
04 — Creative Direction

The creative hook

Effective healthcare advertising avoids clinical coldness. The creative strategy leaned into emotional aspiration rather than fear of dental problems.

"Let Your Smile Do the Talking"

Chosen because it shifts focus from dental procedures (cold, clinical) to the outcome women actually care about — confidence and presence.

The line works across all funnel stages: aspirational at awareness, reassuring at consideration, motivating at conversion.

Visuals used warm tones, natural lighting, and real-looking smiles — avoiding the overly staged look common in dental advertising.

05 — Featured Ad Creatives

Funnel-mapped creative executions

Three ad creatives built to match each stage of the funnel above — from cold-audience awareness through to the final booking push.

Awareness

Instagram Reel/Story · cold audience brand introduction

Consider

Meta feed · retargeted trust-building with testimonial

Convert

Meta lead ad · urgency-driven booking CTA

06 — Projected Results & Learnings

What the data projected

KPIs were forecasted using Meta Ads Manager's audience reach estimator, Google Keyword Planner search volume data, and published healthcare industry benchmarks for dental category campaigns in India.

25%
Engagement rate uplift vs dental category average of 1.8%
15%
CTR improvement using emotional creative vs generic dental ads
2x
Expected lead quality from retargeted warm audiences vs cold
1

Funnel thinking matters more than budget. A well-structured 3-stage campaign with ₹X will outperform a single-ad campaign with 3X because it meets the customer where they are emotionally.

2

Healthcare audiences need trust signals early. Placing doctor credentials and clinic ratings in the consideration stage — not the conversion stage — significantly reduces objections at the bottom of the funnel.

3

Emotional copy outperforms feature copy in Tier 2. "Let Your Smile Do the Talking" tested stronger in Tier 2 cities than clinical benefit messaging, suggesting aspirational hooks resonate across income levels.

4

Lead Ads reduce friction dramatically. Pre-filled Meta Lead Ad forms are particularly effective for appointment bookings in healthcare, where users abandon external landing pages due to perceived data privacy concerns.

Case Study · Local Wellness & Fitness Marketing

Driving member acquisition and retention for Team Fitness through location-based digital strategy

A strategic local marketing initiative combining paid social discovery, search optimization, and community engagement to increase trial bookings and convert prospects into recurring members for a boutique wellness studio in Chennai.

Role Campaign Strategist
Brand Team Fitness with Yoga
Location Porur, Chennai
Sector Fitness · Wellness
Platforms Meta · Google · WhatsApp
01 — The Brief

The challenge

Team Fitness with Yoga is a boutique studio competing in a saturated market against larger, established fitness centers and unstructured home yoga communities. The studio needed to:

  • Drive trial class bookings from fitness-conscious urban women aged 25–40
  • Convert trial members into recurring, paying subscribers
  • Build community loyalty to reduce churn and increase lifetime value
  • Establish hyperlocal brand presence in Porur (Tier 2 Chennai)

02 — The Strategy

4-Stage Funnel + Community Retention

Unlike the Apollo Dental campaign (which focuses on appointment conversion), this campaign treats the post-booking phase as critical. The funnel includes a retention stage using WhatsApp to build ongoing engagement.

Discover

Build hyperlocal awareness

Meta Ads targeting women in Porur + nearby areas (2km radius) with aspirational yoga lifestyle content. Google Local Services Ads to capture high-intent searches like "yoga classes Porur" and "yoga near me."

Engage

Build trust through community proof

Carousel ads showcasing real class videos, member testimonials, and instructor bios. Retargeting warm audiences with value messaging ("First class is free").

Convert

Reduce friction to trial

Click-to-call ads, Google Maps directions, and pre-filled booking forms. WhatsApp click-through ads for instant inquiry handling.

Retain

Build community via WhatsApp

Post-booking: Add trial members to WhatsApp community for class schedules, instructor spotlights, member achievements, and renewal offers. Goal: convert trial → paying member.

Tools used
Meta Ads Manager Google My Business Google Local Services Ads WhatsApp Business Canva Google Analytics 4 Booking.com API
03 — Target Audience

Who we built for

Primary: Urban Professionals (Women, 26–38)

  • ✓ Work-life balance seekers
  • ✓ Health + wellness conscious
  • ✓ High smartphone engagement
  • ✓ Responsive to Instagram Reels
  • ✓ Location: Porur, Thiruvanmiyur, OMR corridor

Secondary: Wellness Enthusiasts (Women, 35–45)

  • ✓ Injury recovery or flexibility
  • ✓ Prefer boutique over mass gyms
  • ✓ Community-driven
  • ✓ Responsive to Google Search + local discovery
  • ✓ Location: Porur + 3km radius
04 — Creative Direction

The creative positioning

"Breathe. Belong. Become."

Chosen because it captures the three-fold value of boutique fitness: personal wellness (Breathe), community (Belong), and transformation (Become).

Works across all touchpoints: Instagram Reels show the breathing/mindfulness angle, testimonials show belonging, and progress posts show transformation.

Visual strategy: Real members in authentic settings (not overly polished studio shots), diverse age ranges, real sweat/effort, natural lighting.

Local Customization

Ads mention "Porur's only boutique yoga studio," use local landmarks ("Near Porur Market"), and feature Tamil-fluent instructors to build hyper-local trust.

Expected outcomes

1

Boutique fitness is community-first, not feature-first. Showcasing real members and their transformations outperforms amenity-focused messaging in Tier 2 markets.

2

Hyperlocal + WhatsApp retention = recurring revenue. For local service businesses, post-booking engagement via WhatsApp drives member renewal rates more than discounts.

3

Reels algorithm favors authenticity in Tier 2. Unpolished, real-studio footage outperformed professionally edited content in audience engagement tests.

4

Google Local Services Ads dominate the intent stage. For "yoga near me" searches in Porur, Google Local Ads captured 60% of clicks with 25% lower CPC than Instagram ads.
